# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Native macOS 26 on Apple Silicon. The skill's platform enum has no macOS value; this app follows macOS conventions. [Certain]

## Stack

Swift, SwiftUI settings/history, AppKit menu bar and a nonactivating recording panel; FluidAudio/Core ML transcription through the pinned local helper. [Certain]

## Users and purpose

People dictating English into existing Mac applications. Success means recording from a global shortcut, saving recoverable output, then inserting into the original focused field without sending a form/message. [Certain]

## Operating context

Local-first inference, model selection, tap/hold shortcuts, no Dock icon, optional menu bar item, Finder reopening, private history, clear permission and model-recovery states. [Certain]

## Capabilities and constraints

The selected local model is Parakeet Unified. M1 performance validation is deferred; publish measured configurations only. Reject known secure fields, retain uncertain output, and never automatically retry uncertain insertion. Cloud, cleanup, dictionary learning, full model management, signing/notarization and releases remain separate work. [Certain]

## Visual commitment

The PRD requires a minimal liquid-glass bubble near the destination display's bottom edge. Native settings/history should prioritize familiar controls, clear states, and recovery. [Certain]

## Evidence on hand

Recorded cross-app insertion/observation matrix in spike/FEASIBILITY.md; real local model and connected synthetic microphone checks in module-one/session/README.md. Synthetic speech evidence does not prove real-user quality or first-word preservation. [Certain]

## Accessibility

Respect Reduce Motion, Reduce Transparency, Increase Contrast, and VoiceOver. The recording bubble must not steal focus; state and recovery are also visible in settings/history. [Certain]
